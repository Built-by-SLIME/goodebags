Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate the games:

  • Public wallet address: Used for NFT/token ownership verification and leaderboard tracking.
  • Username: Voluntarily provided for leaderboard display. Can be an @X handle.
  • Gameplay data: Scores, win/loss records, and game settings.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or store:

  • Private keys, seed phrases, or wallet passwords
  • Personal identification documents
  • Payment or banking information

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for:

  • Verifying NFT ownership for game access
  • Displaying leaderboard rankings
  • Improving game performance and user experience

4. Blockchain Data

NFT ownership verification is performed by querying public blockchain data (XRPL and Hedera networks). This data is publicly available on-chain and not controlled by Goodebags Games.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to maintain wallet connection sessions (WalletConnect, Xaman). We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Wallet connection data is processed by the respective wallet providers (WalletConnect, Xaman) according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Retention

Leaderboard scores and usernames are retained indefinitely unless you request deletion. Wallet addresses are retained as long as you maintain an active account or score on the leaderboard.

8. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your username and scores by contacting us via X / Twitter or Discord.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
