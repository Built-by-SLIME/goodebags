# Ape-Mod-X — Official Rules

## Players
2 – 4 Players (or more with additional decks)

---

## Setup

1. Shuffle the cards and split them equally between players.
2. If the cards don't divide evenly, place any spare cards on the table — they become a bonus for the winner of the first round.
3. Hold your cards in front of you so that you can only see the front (character) of the top card.

---

## Gameplay

4. After deciding who will start, the first player chooses a **trait category** of the character on their top card that they believe will win them the round, and they give/call out that trait category (or the colour it's backed by) and their character's value for it.
   - e.g. **"Wear & Hold - 6"** or **"Green - 6"**

5. The other players must then say their value for that trait, but then also call out and add on the value of their **XTRA trait**, if any.

6. The **XTRA 6th trait** is a bonus and can only be used/added in defence — when you are **not** the 1st player that nominated the trait category called in this round.

7. The player with the **highest score wins the round** and wins the other player's card(s).

---

## Ties & Frozen Cards

8. In the event of a trait score tie (when extra included in defence), tied cards are **'frozen out'** and left on the table (alongside any beaten cards by a 3rd or 4th player).

9. The players that tied repeat the round with the same player nominating the trait to play from the next card, and the winner takes **all** of the cards including any 'frozen' ones.

10. The winner places cards won at the bottom of their stack and then selects/calls a trait and value from their next card on top.

11. If a player's last card is frozen because of a tie, they **automatically lose the game** as they have no active cards left.

---

## Winning

12. This continues until one player has won all of the cards being used in the game — they are the **winner**.
    **Shuffle and play again!**
