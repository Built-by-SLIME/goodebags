# The Bee's Knees (TBK) — Official Rules

## Players
1 – 4 Players (or more with additional decks)

---

## Setup

1. Shuffle the cards and split them equally between players.
2. If the cards don't divide evenly, place the spare cards on the table — they become a bonus for the winner of the first round.
3. Place your cards in front of you in a pile with faces down.

---

## Gameplay

4. After deciding who will start, the first player chooses a **trait** from the character on their top card that they believe will win them the round, and they give/call out that trait and their character's value for it.

5. The second player must then say the value that they have for that trait.

6. The player with the **highest score wins the round** and wins the other player's card(s).

7. The winner places cards won at the bottom of their stack and then selects/calls a trait and value from their next card on top.

---

## Ties & Frozen Cards

7. In the event of a trait score tie, both cards are **frozen out** and left on the table (alongside beaten cards by a 3rd or 4th player). The players that tied repeat the round and the winner takes all of the cards.

8. If a player's last card is frozen because of a tie, they **automatically lose the game** as they have no active cards left.

---

## Winning

9. This continues until one player has won all of the cards being used in the game — they are the **winner!**
